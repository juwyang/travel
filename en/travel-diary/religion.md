Judaism and Christianity\
Abrahamic monotheism, unlike systems such as those of Zeus or Odin. The earliest Christian church was more like a "Nazarene sect" within Judaism. The biggest disagreement between the two lies in the idea of the Messiah, the saviour: Judaism has no instance of him yet, while Christianity holds that the Messiah is Jesus.\
The Chinese word for "Christ": Hebrew Messiah → Greek Christos (Χριστός) → Chinese transliteration Jilisidu → shortened to Jidu.\
The name Jesus comes from Greek; in Hebrew it is Joshua (meaning "saviour"), made up of the Hebrew "Yahweh (YHWH)" and "to save (Yasha)", literally "Yahweh is salvation" or "the Lord saves".\
Jesus is the "anointed one" of Yahweh, his incarnation, come to save.\
It began as Jewish stories, the Tanakh (Old Testament): Creation, the Flood, the Exodus, King David.
